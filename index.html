Washington · CFTCCFTC — Commodity Futures Trading Commission. US derivatives regulator. Registers the venues that list event contracts (prediction markets) and can bar a contract class from listing or clearing on a public-interest finding. · Prediction markets

The regulator has proposed to delist the exact instrument this desk uses to price geopolitics

A Federal Register sweep on the Hormuz file returned something better than a shipping notice: the CFTC's proposed rule Prediction Markets; Public Interest Determinations (RIN 3038-AF65, published 12 June 2026, comments closed 27 July, 17 CFR Part 40). It sets out when an event contract "involves" an enumerated activity such as war or terrorism, and may therefore be found contrary to the public interest and barred from listing or clearing on any CFTC-registered venue.

The rule text works through Hormuz explicitly. A contract on whether Iran initiates armed conflict in the Strait, or whether a named non-state actor attacks shipping there, is described as involving war or terrorism because the settlement-determining occurrence sits inside the enumerated activity itself. A contract on whether a specified volume of crude transits the Strait is drawn as the contrast case. The carve-out is volumetric, and that is a precise instruction: the priced instrument for a chokepoint survives only if it settles on throughput rather than on the act.

This is a live exposure for the desk rather than a curiosity. The Fed strip quoted above is a Kalshi product; the Hormuz and CLARITY odds carried in this monitor for weeks are the same asset class. Nothing in the Fed or legislative series touches an enumerated activity, so the rule as proposed does not reach them. The conflict-outcome contracts are the ones with a listing risk, and they are the ones the geopolitics desk would otherwise reach for first. Comment period closed seventeen days ago and no final rule has published; that gap is the thing to watch, not the proposal.

Actionable: Treat volumetric and throughput contracts as the durable geopolitical expression and stop building reads on conflict-outcome contracts. Tracking call: no final rule under RIN 3038-AF65 publishes in the Federal Register on or before 15 Sep.
