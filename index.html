Westinghouse filed two and a half years before it had to — that is the owners telling you where the mark is

Westinghouse Electric confidentially submitted a draft Form S-1 today. Cameco owns 49% and Brookfield Renewable 51%, bought together for $7.9B in 2023. The relevant clause is not in the filing: under last year's arrangement with Washington, the US government holds a participation interest and can require an IPO on or before January 2029 if the company is worth $30B or more, converting into roughly an 8% shareholder. You do not file into a forced-IPO trigger unless you are already at or through it.
