Three shocks landed at once: MSCI's review excluded Korea from its Developed-Markets reclassification, US tech fell overnight, and leveraged AI positions unwound after a year in which the KOSPI had doubled (+110% YTD).

The derivative read mainstream coverage misses: this did not stay in Asia. A synchronized, single-factor drawdown across Seoul, Taipei and Philadelphia is a positioning event, not six separate fundamental stories. The tell that it was a flush: every proxy is bouncing pre-market (EWY +4.6%, SMH +1.0%) — the bid returned once forced selling cleared.

Actionable: Treat the Asia complex as one trade, not a contagion to fade name-by-name. The bounce is on probation until Micron tonight validates demand; a weak print re-opens the downside, a strong one confirms the flush is over.

Macro · Rates · FOMC

The hawkish Fed is the accelerant under the whole unwind — a +15bp repricing of the 2Y is what drains the most crowded trade first. But oil is collapsing, so the dots may be fighting the last war.

At Kevin Warsh's first FOMC the Fed held 3.50–3.75%, but the dots turned hawkish — 9 of 18 participants now flag at least one hike in 2026, only one a cut. The front end repriced for it: the 2Y jumped ~15bp to 4.24%, the 10Y sits at 4.51%, and 2s10s flattened to +0.34. Tighter expected policy is precisely the force that pulls capital out of the highest-multiple, most-leveraged trade first — which this week was AI/memory in Seoul, Taipei and Philadelphia.

The derivative tell: the inflation input behind the hawkish dots is already reversing. WTI has fallen ~11% to $84.65 on Iran de-escalation — a disinflationary force the 2Y's hike-pricing has not absorbed. The rates market is positioning for last month's inflation; the oil market is pricing next month's. A soft PCE plus stable-lower oil is the setup to fade 2Y hike pricing — the same dovish pivot that would relieve both the AI complex and Bitcoin.

Actionable: Watch core PCE (~Jun 26) against the Fed's raised bogey and the 2Y into late July. Fade 2Y hike pricing on a soft PCE + lower oil; that pivot is the shared upside for semis and BTC alike.
