Washington · SEC / CFTC · Crypto

The deregulation desk is open: SEC unwinds climate rules while the CLARITY Act races a July 4 deadline.

The SEC moved on two fronts this week — proposing to rescind the contested corporate climate-disclosure rules and floating capital-offering reforms to ease access for smaller issuers. The bigger market-structure prize is the CLARITY Act: it cleared Senate Banking 15–9 on May 14, hit the legislative calendar June 1, and the White House is targeting July 4 for signature. Polymarket prices 2026 passage at 59%; the gate is ~7 Democratic crossovers for 60 Senate votes.

Actionable: CLARITY is an under-owned BTC catalyst hiding inside the macro gloom — a spot-market regime change for digital commodities that pairs directly with the capitulation setup above. Track the Senate floor calendar into the July 4 target; a vote schedule is the trigger, a slip past the recess is the fade.
