The pain is mechanical: a higher discount rate compresses every long-duration risk asset (AI, SpaceX, BTC) simultaneously, which is exactly the cross-asset signature on the tape today.

Actionable: The Fed-hold is now consensus; the asymmetry is in a cooler June print (early July) that revives cut-hope and lifts duration risk. Mark the 2-year at 4.10% — a break back below 3.90% is the tell that the "no cuts" narrative is cracking. A T5YIFR move above 2.35% would be the genuine warning the unwind is missing.

Actionable: The mispriced tail is energy, not equities — long-dated WTI calls / energy equity are cheap optionality on a talks collapse, since crude is discounting a clean resolution. Defense (ITA) is the cleaner directional long; oil below $75 would confirm the de-escalation, a break back above $90 the warning the MOU is failing.
