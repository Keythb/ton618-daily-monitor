The senator-by-senator board prices 1.85 of the seven Democratic votes cloture needs — and the market is more worried about Republican defections than it lets on

Cloture on the motion to proceed ripens 2:15pm ET on 15 September and needs 60 votes against 53 Republican seats — seven Democratic or independent crossovers, minimum. The part nobody writes up is on the other side of the aisle. The same board quotes three Republicans at 15–16% to vote yea: Mike Lee 16.0%, Hawley 16.0%, Moran 15.0%.
