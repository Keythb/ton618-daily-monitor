MSCI adopted the rule that fails Strategy, and the wrapper gave back its whole rally day — the coin barely moved

MSCI said on 15 Oct that non-operating companies will be ineligible for its Global Investable Market Indexes, effective with the November review at the close of 30 Nov. Issuers first face a screen for “substantial” operating assets (more than 50% of total assets). Those that fail get five financial-ratio tests, and four flags means exclusion.
